You have a paper.
You need six minutes of film.

Founders and researchers are now expected to be media-first — personally. Most have no crew, no budget, and no idea where to start. The production cost of good work has collapsed; the judgment it takes has not.

We produce for the people we cover, and we teach them to produce for themselves. It funds the publication and it fills the pipeline.
